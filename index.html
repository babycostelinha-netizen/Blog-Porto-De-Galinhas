01 / My Travel Experience

A trip to remember

Last year, I traveled to Porto de Galinhas with my family. We visited the beautiful beaches and natural pools. We took a boat ride on a colorful jangada. I saw many colorful fish and enjoyed the amazing ocean. We also ate delicious seafood at a local restaurant. It was an unforgettable trip!
